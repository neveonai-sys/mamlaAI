Account & Registration

Q1: Can I register if I'm not a lawyer? ▼

A: Yes. Mamla.AI serves three user types:

  • Lawyers and advocates (Vakil plans)
  • Law firms and chambers (Nyaya Firm plans)
  • Citizens/litigants (Nagrik plans)
Q2: What do I need to register? ▼
  • Valid email address and phone number
  • Password (strong, unique)
  • If a lawyer: Bar Council registration number
  • If a citizen: Basic identity (name, email — no special credentials)
Q3: Can I delete my account? ▼

A: Yes. Log in → Account Settings → Delete Account. Most data is deleted within 30 days, except legally required records (tax, court orders) which are retained for 7 years.

Q4: What if I forgot my password? ▼

A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and we'll send a reset link (valid for 1 hour).

Payment & Billing

Q5: What payment methods do you accept? ▼
  • Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  • UPI (BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Netbanking and digital wallets

All payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS certified payment gateway.

Q6: Is GST included in the price? ▼

A: No. GST is calculated and added at checkout based on your location. You'll see the total before confirming payment.

Q7: What is your refund policy? ▼
  • Monthly plans: No refund for unused portions (non-refundable)
  • Annual plans: Refund within 7 days if service hasn't been used
  • Service failures: Issue as credit toward future services
Q8: What is your free trial? ▼
  • Lawyers: 30-day free trial (all Starter features)
  • Citizens: 7-day free trial (basic case lookup + 5 legal chats)

Trials are non-renewable. After expiry, upgrade to a paid plan.

AI Drafting & Legal Documents

Q9: Is AI-generated content legally valid? ▼

A: AI-generated content is only valid if reviewed and approved by a licensed advocate, signed/notarized as per court requirements, and compliant with procedural rules of your court. Never file AI-generated documents without advocate review.

Q10: What documents can I draft? ▼
  • Bail applications, anticipatory bail
  • Court petitions (civil, criminal, PIL)
  • Affidavits and declarations
  • Legal notices and demand letters
  • Wills and agreements
  • RTI applications
Q11: Is the AI trained on Indian law? ▼

A: Yes. Our AI is trained on Indian Penal Code (IPC), Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), Code of Civil Procedure (CPC), Indian Evidence Act, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Supreme Court and High Court judgments, and court-specific rules. We still recommend advocate review for court-specific nuances.

Q12: What if the court rejects my Mamla.AI draft? ▼

A: That's your responsibility. We don't cover re-filing fees, court fines, or damages. Always have your advocate review before filing.

eCourts Integration

Q13: How do I look up my case? ▼

A: Log in → eCourts Lookup → Enter your CNR number (or case number + court + year). Results show case status, next hearing date, and order PDFs.

Q14: How often is eCourts data updated? ▼

A: We sync with eCourts daily (overnight, 2-4 AM IST). Data is typically 1-7 days old depending on court uploads. Always verify critical dates by calling the court directly.

Privacy & Data Security

Q15: Is my data secure? ▼

A: We use AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, role-based access control, and regular security audits. We can't guarantee 100% security, but we implement industry best practices.

Q16: Is my data sold to third parties? ▼

A: No. We do NOT sell personal data. We share anonymized, aggregated data with service providers under confidentiality agreements, and with law enforcement only via court order. See Privacy Policy for details.

Q17: How do I opt out of marketing emails? ▼

A: Click "Unsubscribe" in any email, or go to Account Settings → Notifications → Marketing Emails → Off.

Legal & Compliance

Q20: Are you a law firm? ▼

A: No. Mamla.AI is a legal technology platform. We do NOT provide legal advice or legal services. You must consult a licensed advocate for legal advice.

Q21: Is Mamla.AI liable if I lose my case? ▼

A: No. Courts decide cases based on facts and law. Mamla.AI makes no guarantee about case outcomes. You accept all legal risk.

Q22: Do you comply with GDPR? ▼

A: Yes (for EU users). Your data is processed per GDPR standards. You have rights to access, correct, and delete data. Contact privacy@mamla.ai for GDPR requests.

Q23: What if I have a privacy complaint? ▼

A: Email grievance@mamla.ai with details. We respond within 7 business days. Escalate to dpo@mamla.ai if unresolved.
