Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 20th July 2026
Effective Date: 25th July 2026

Summary: We use cookies to keep the site working, personalize your experience, and measure usage. You are in control - you can accept all, decline non-essential cookies, or manage your preferences at any time.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the service. We also use similar technologies like localStorage and session storage for the same purposes.

2. How We Use Cookies

Cookies used by Mamla.AI may be first-party or third-party cookies and may be either session-based or persistent, depending on their purpose and duration.

We use cookies in four categories:

2.1 Necessary Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are required for the Platform to function. They cannot be disabled.

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These help us understand how users interact with Mamla.AI so we can improve the product. We use PostHog (EU region, GDPR compliant) for analytics. No mouse movements, keystrokes, or sensitive content is captured - only business events like feature opens and page views.

2.3 Marketing Cookies (Optional)

These allow us to show relevant product updates and offers. Currently we do not use third-party advertising networks. If this changes, we will update this policy and require fresh consent.

2.4 Personalization Cookies (Optional)

These remember your in-app preferences such as sidebar state, theme, and recent searches, so you don't have to reconfigure on every visit.

3. Managing Your Preferences

When you first visit Mamla.AI, a cookie consent banner appears. You can:

  • Accept all cookies - enables all four categories.
  • Manage preferences - choose which optional categories to allow.
  • Reject optional cookies - only necessary cookies will be set.

Users may review or modify their cookie preferences at any time through the Cookie Preferences Centre available within the Platform, without affecting the continued use of necessary cookies. You can also email support@mamla.ai to update your consent record on our server.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not allow third-party advertising networks to set cookies on Mamla.AI. The only third-party analytics tool is PostHog, which is configured with:

  • autocapture: false - no automatic mouse/scroll/hover capture
  • capture_pageview: false - manual page view tracking only
  • disable_session_recording: true - screen recording is off
  • EU data residency - your data never leaves the EU PostHog servers

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR and India's DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Know what cookies and trackers are active on your device.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Request deletion of analytics data associated with your session or account.

For further information regarding your privacy rights, including requests for access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we change the tools or technologies we use. Material changes will be communicated via email and/or a new consent prompt in the app.

7. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Policy, your personal data, or cookie preferences, please contact us at support@mamla.ai.
