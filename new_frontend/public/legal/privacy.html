Last Updated: May 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under Indian and applicable international data protection laws.

1. Introduction

Neveon Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us", "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

We act as a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India. We comply with:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) — Sections 5 (purpose limitation), 6 (consent), 7–9 (Data Principal rights), 10 (retention), 11 (Data Fiduciary obligations).
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) — Section 43A (reasonable security practices) and Section 72A (breach of confidentiality).
  • IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules) — governing collection and handling of sensitive personal data.
  • Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TRAI) — for promotional communications.
  • GDPR — applicable to users in the European Economic Area.

2a. Consent and Legal Basis for Processing (DPDP Act)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the following lawful bases:

  • Explicit Consent (DPDP Section 6): You provide free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent before we collect your data. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting privacy@mamla.ai or via account settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Deemed Consent (DPDP Section 7): For processing necessary to perform a contract you have entered into (e.g., delivering the legal drafting service you subscribed to), or for compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate Use: For security, fraud prevention, and system integrity — limited to what is necessary and proportionate.

We do not use your personal data for any purpose other than those stated in this Policy. Any new purpose requires fresh consent.

6. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, Sections 7–13) and applicable laws, you have the following rights. Indian users may exercise rights under the DPDP Act; EU users may additionally exercise rights under GDPR.

6.1 Right to Access and Portability (DPDP Section 7 / GDPR Articles 15, 20)

  • Right to know what personal data we hold about you and the purposes for which it is being processed.
  • Right to receive a summary of your data and processing activities.
  • Right to download your data in a portable format (JSON) via GET /api/users/privacy/export-data/.
  • Submit requests within 30 days via privacy@mamla.ai.

6.2 Right to Correction and Completion (DPDP Section 8 / GDPR Article 16)

  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Update your account details anytime in your profile settings.
  • For data we hold that you cannot self-correct, contact privacy@mamla.ai.

6.3 Right to Erasure (DPDP Section 9 / GDPR Article 17)

  • Right to request deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose collected, or when you withdraw consent.
  • Submit a deletion request via POST /api/users/privacy/delete-data/ or email privacy@mamla.ai.
  • Exceptions: Data retained under legal obligations (e.g., financial records under Income Tax Act, legal records under applicable law) will be anonymized rather than deleted.

6.4 Right to Grievance Redressal (DPDP Section 13)

  • Right to lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer within 30 days of the issue.
  • If unresolved, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India once constituted under the DPDP Act.
  • EU users may lodge complaints with their local Data Protection Authority.

6.5 Right to Withdraw Consent (DPDP Section 6)

  • You may withdraw consent for marketing or analytics processing at any time via account settings or by contacting privacy@mamla.ai.
  • Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

6.6 Right to Object to Processing (GDPR Article 21 — EU Users)

  • Right to object to processing for direct marketing or profiling.
  • Opt-out via account settings or email privacy@mamla.ai.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • Encryption at rest (AES-256).
  • Role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Regular security audits.
  • Employee confidentiality agreements.

No security system is 100% impenetrable. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential. If a personal data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

  • Notify the Data Protection Board of India as required under the DPDP Act, 2023 and Section 43A of the IT Act, 2000.
  • Notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach via email to your registered address.
  • Provide details of the nature of the breach, categories of data affected, steps taken, and remediation measures.

Per Indian TRAI regulations and GDPR, we require your explicit consent before sending marketing emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages. Every promotional message includes an unsubscribe link. You can also update preferences in account settings or email support@mamla.ai.
