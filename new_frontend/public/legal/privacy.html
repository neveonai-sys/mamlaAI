Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026
Effective Date: July 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under Indian and applicable international data protection laws.

1. Introduction

Neveon Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us", "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

We act as a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India. We comply with:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) - Sections 5 (purpose limitation), 6 (consent), 7–9 (Data Principal rights), 10 (retention), 11 (Data Fiduciary obligations).
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) - Section 43A (reasonable security practices) and Section 72A (breach of confidentiality).
  • IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules) - governing collection and handling of sensitive personal data.
  • Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TRAI) - for promotional communications.
  • GDPR - applicable to users in the European Economic Area.

2a. Consent and Legal Basis for Processing (DPDP Act)

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the following lawful bases:

  • Explicit Consent (DPDP Section 6): You provide free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent before we collect your data. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting support@mamla.ai or via account settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Deemed Consent (DPDP Section 7): For processing necessary to perform a contract you have entered into (e.g., delivering the legal drafting service you subscribed to), or for compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate Use: For security, fraud prevention, and system integrity - limited to what is necessary and proportionate.

We do not use your personal data for any purpose other than those stated in this Policy. Any new purpose requires fresh consent.

2b. Children's Privacy (new clause)

Our Services are intended for use only by individuals who are at least 18 years old (or the age of legal majority in their home jurisdiction) and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal data of a child (an individual under 18 years, or as separately defined for EU users under Article 8 GDPR) without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, obtained through the mechanisms prescribed under Rule 10 of the DPDP Rules, 2025 (including approved digital identity verification methods). We do not carry out behavioural monitoring, profiling, or targeted advertising directed at children. If we learn that a child's data has been collected without such verified consent, we will delete it promptly, except where retention is separately required by law.

6. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, Sections 7–13) and applicable laws, you have the following rights. Indian users may exercise rights under the DPDP Act; EU users may additionally exercise rights under GDPR.

6.1 Right to Access and Portability (DPDP Section 7 / GDPR Articles 15, 20) (updated)

You may request access to your personal data anytime. You can request a copy or summary of your personal data and how it is processed via the export tool (GET /api/users/privacy/export-data/) or by emailing support@mamla.ai. We will acknowledge your request promptly and provide a substantive response within one month of receipt (extendable by up to two further months for complex requests, with notice explaining the delay), consistent with our obligations under the DPDP Act, 2023 and, where applicable, Article 12(3) GDPR. This is distinct from our grievance-redressal process (Section 9 below), under which we ask that complaints about our handling of your data be raised within 30 days of the issue arising so we can investigate promptly.

6.2 Right to Correction and Completion (DPDP Section 8 / GDPR Article 16)

  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Update your account details anytime in your profile settings.
  • For data we hold that you cannot self-correct, contact support@mamla.ai.

6.3 Right to Erasure (DPDP Section 9 / GDPR Article 17)

  • Right to request deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose collected, or when you withdraw consent.
  • Submit a deletion request via POST /api/users/privacy/delete-data/ or email support@mamla.ai.
  • Exceptions: Data retained under legal obligations (e.g., financial records under Income Tax Act, legal records under applicable law) will be anonymized rather than deleted.

6.4 Right to Grievance Redressal (DPDP Section 13)

  • Right to lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer within 30 days of the issue.
  • If unresolved, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India once constituted under the DPDP Act.
  • EU users may lodge complaints with their local Data Protection Authority.

6.5 Right to Withdraw Consent (DPDP Section 6)

  • You may withdraw consent for marketing or analytics processing at any time via account settings or by contacting support@mamla.ai
  • Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

6.6 Right to Object to Processing (GDPR Article 21 - EU Users)

  • Right to object to processing for direct marketing or profiling.
  • Opt-out via account settings or email support@mamla.ai

6.7 Right to Nominate (DPDP Section 14) (new clause)

You may nominate another individual to exercise your rights under this Policy and applicable law in the event of your death or incapacity, in accordance with Section 14 of the DPDP Act, 2023. This right applies to users in India; it has no equivalent for users in the European Economic Area, where succession to personal-data rights is instead governed by applicable national law. To register or update a nomination, contact support@mamla.ai

7. Data Security (updated)

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Data masking and anonymisation/pseudonymisation techniques where appropriate to the processing context.
  • Regular security audits and monitoring.
  • Retention of access and activity logs for a minimum of one year.
  • A documented incident-response process.
  • Contractual requirements on our data processors (including Razorpay, Supabase, and PostHog) to implement safeguards equivalent to our own.
  • Employee confidentiality agreements.

This is in accordance with Rule 6 of the DPDP Rules, 2025 and Article 32 GDPR.

No security system is 100% impenetrable. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India as soon as we become aware of it, and will notify affected Data Principals within 72 hours of that Board notification, in line with Rule 7 of the DPDP Rules, 2025. Where the breach concerns users in the European Economic Area and is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms, we will also notify the competent EU supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware and inform affected individuals without undue delay. Notifications will describe the nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of individuals and records affected, likely consequences, and the remedial measures taken or proposed.
