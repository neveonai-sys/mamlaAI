6. Your Rights

6.1 Access and Portability (GDPR Articles 15, 20)

  • Right to know what data we hold about you.
  • Right to download your data in portable format (JSON, CSV).
  • Request within 30 days via privacy@mamla.ai.

6.2 Correction and Rectification (GDPR Article 16)

  • Right to correct inaccurate data.
  • Update your account details anytime in your profile.

6.3 Erasure (GDPR Article 17 - "Right to be Forgotten")

  • Right to request deletion of your data.
  • Exceptions: Legal obligations, ongoing disputes, essential for service.

6.4 Object to Processing (GDPR Article 21)

  • Right to object to marketing/profiling.
  • Opt-out via account settings or email.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3).
  • Encryption at rest (AES-256).
  • Role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Regular security audits.
  • Employee confidentiality agreements.

No security system is 100% impenetrable. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours via email.

8. Promotional Communications

Per Indian TRAI regulations and GDPR, we require your explicit consent before sending marketing emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages. Every promotional message includes an unsubscribe link. You can also update preferences in account settings or email support@mamla.ai.

9. Grievance Redressal

Grievance Officer: grievance@mamla.ai
Address: Neveon Technologies Private Limited, Siliguri, West Bengal, India

Submit complaints within 30 days of the issue. We respond within 7 business days. Escalate unresolved issues to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@mamla.ai.

10. International Data Transfers

If you are in the EU, your data is processed under GDPR. Analytics data is stored in PostHog's EU region. Transfers to India are authorized under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). You have EU remedies under GDPR.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy anytime. Material changes will be notified via email 30 days before the update. Continued use after notification constitutes acceptance.
