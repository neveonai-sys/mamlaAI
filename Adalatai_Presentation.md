## Slide 5 — What Mamla.AI Does (Plain Language)

Think of Mamla.AI as a **three-layer system**:

### Layer 1 — Case Command Centre
Everything about a case in one place:
- Create a case file, add the client, link the eCourts CNR number
- All documents, drafts, calendar events, and hearing notes live under that case
- A unique **Mamla Case ID** (e.g. `MC-2026-A3XF9`) so nothing gets mixed up
- Works for District Court and High Court cases

### Layer 2 — AI Assistant (The "Second Brain")
The AI that actually helps, not just answers:
- **Guided Drafting:** Talk to the AI, answer its questions, get a full petition/affidavit/application — tailored to your specific case
- **Hearing Prep Agent:** Night before a hearing, the AI reads your case history, past orders, and case notes — and gives you a briefing on what to say
- **Document Intelligence:** Upload any court document or order — the AI extracts the key facts, deadlines, and action points
- **TalkDoc (Chat with your files):** Ask questions about any uploaded document in plain language

### Layer 3 — Court Integration (eCourts)
Live court data without manual searching:
- Look up any case by CNR number — District Court and High Court
- See past hearings, next date, order PDFs
- Cause list for your court — see your cases for the day
- Advocate-wise and party-wise search

## Slide 6 — The "Guided Drafting" Difference

**This is our biggest differentiator. No competitor does this.**

### Old Way (Every Other Tool)
Fill a form → Get a generic draft → Fix it manually for 2 hours

### Mamla.AI Way
1. Lawyer says: "I need a bail application"
2. AI asks: "What is the nature of the offence?" — one question at a time
3. AI asks: "Has the accused been in custody before?" — it knows what matters
4. After 5–8 focused questions, AI has everything it needs
5. Lawyer reviews the **Draft Plan** (a summary card showing what will be written)
6. Lawyer clicks "Generate" — gets a complete, accurate, court-ready draft

**The draft is not generic. It is built from the actual facts of the actual case.**

If the lawyer already has documents uploaded, the AI reads them first and skips questions it can already answer. If the case is in the system, the AI pre-fills everything it knows.

## Appendix B — Objection Handling

**"We already use ChatGPT for drafting"**
> ChatGPT does not know your case. It does not know your client. It does not read eCourts orders. Mamla.AI's guided drafting uses the actual facts of your actual case — the output is a petition, not a template.

**"We have a junior for this work"**
> Your junior takes 2 hours on a bail application. Mamla.AI takes 10 minutes and the output is better because it does not miss clauses. Your junior can focus on research and court appearances instead.

**"Is my data safe?"**
> Yes. All data is encrypted. Login is handled by Supabase — the same technology used by companies like GitHub and Mozilla. We do not store passwords. Your case files are private to your account.

**"What if internet is down in court?"**
> Mamla.AI is primarily a preparation tool — you use it the night before or between hearings. The drafts you generate can be saved locally. The hearing brief is readable offline once downloaded.

**"₹699 seems expensive"**
> A single bail application drafted by a junior associate costs ₹500–₹2,000 in staff time. Mamla.AI generates 70 drafts a month for ₹699. The tool pays for itself on day one.
