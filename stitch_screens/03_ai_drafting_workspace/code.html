Employment Agreement

THIS EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of this 24th day of May, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Mamla Tech Solutions Private Limited, a corporation organized under the laws of the State (the "Employer"), and Johnathan Doe, an individual (the "Employee").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" shall mean any and all information, whether or not in writing, of a private, secret or confidential nature concerning the Employer's business or financial affairs.

1.2 "Intellectual Property" means all inventions, discoveries, developments, methods, processes, compositions, works, and names, whether or not patentable or registrable under copyright or similar laws.

2. POSITION AND DUTIES

2.1 Position. The Employee is engaged as the Senior Legal Counsel. In this capacity, the Employee shall perform all duties and responsibilities as are customary for such position and as may be reasonably assigned by the Board of Directors.

2.2 Reporting. The Employee shall report directly to the Chief Executive Officer or such other person as the Employer may designate. The Employee shall devote their full business time and best efforts to the performance of duties for the Employer.

2.3 Best Efforts. During the Employment Period, the Employee shall devote Employee's full business time, attention, skill, and efforts to the faithful performance of Employee's duties hereunder.

3. COMPENSATION

3.1 Base Salary. In consideration of the services to be rendered by Employee, Employer shall pay Employee a base salary at an annual rate of [INSERT AMOUNT].
